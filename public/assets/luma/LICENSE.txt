Luma Works — original procedural 3D assets and analytic studio environment

Created for the Atlas project, 2026. These seven GLB models and studio.hdr
were generated locally with the source scripts in tools/build_luma_assets.py
(Blender 4.3.2) and tools/build_luma_environment.py (Python 3).
No third-party models, photographic HDRIs or external textures were imported.
